## 🎯 Assessment Overview & Scoring Rubric Alignment
This document establishes the defense materials for the Capstone Final Presentation evaluated by the Nedbank Technical Review Panel.

### Component Structure
1. **Problem Statement (5 mins)**: Analysis of customer financial friction, notice deposit complexity, and public LLM hallucination risks.
2. **Technical Architecture Deep-Dive (7 mins)**: Multi-tier breakdown of React 18, Express REST API, Prisma ORM, PostgreSQL 16 on Neon, and JWT auth pipeline.
3. **AI Feature Deep-Dive (8 mins)**: RAG mathematical foundations (chunking, vector generation, cosine similarity scoring, citation injection), and fail-safe local copilot fallbacks.
4. **Live Product Demo (10 mins)**: Step-by-step walkthrough across onboarding, CRUD post management, RAG queries, summarization, and conversation history.
5. **Business Model & Impact (5 mins)**: Contact center deflection metrics, deposit velocity acceleration, and institutional governance.
6. **Technical Q&A Defense (10 mins)**: Panel defense addressing edge cases, security, scaling, and compliance.
